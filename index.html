yt-manager

A desktop tool for managers and creators to handle multiple YouTube channels in one place.

yt-manager is a desktop application that runs locally on your computer. It helps creators and account managers organize multiple YouTube channels, upload content, and schedule publications efficiently.

What it does with YouTube

  • The user connects their YouTube channels by signing in with Google (OAuth). yt-manager only asks for permission to upload videos and to view channel information.
  • For each video, the user sets the title, description, visibility (scheduled, public, unlisted, or private), and whether the video is made for kids, then presses "Subir a YouTube" (Upload to YouTube). Nothing is uploaded without that direct action.
  • Scheduled videos are placed in designated time slots, ensuring proper spacing between publications. To achieve this, yt-manager reads the channel's recent uploads and scheduled times.
  • yt-manager displays the status of managed channels and uploaded videos with direct links to YouTube Studio.

Where your data lives

yt-manager has no servers. It talks directly from your computer to Google's APIs. Your Google authorization and the small amount of channel data it needs are stored only on your computer and can be deleted at any time. See the Privacy Policy for details.

yt-manager uses YouTube API Services. By using its YouTube features you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and your data is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.
